# pyRadPlan – DIM Test (OpenKBP pt_1)

Tests pyRadPlan with one OpenKBP head & neck patient (`pt_1`).  
Reads CSV data, builds a CT + StructureSet, computes the Dose Influence Matrix (DIM),
aggregates it to a `(V × 9)` per-beam matrix, and saves `pt_1/dim.npy`.

## What the script does

1. **Reads voxel spacing** from `pt_1/voxel_dimensions.csv` — x/y/z resolution in mm.

2. **Reads the CT volume** from `pt_1/ct.csv` — decodes flat voxel indices into a 3D HU array `(94 × 128 × 128)`.

3. **Builds a pyRadPlan CT object** — wraps the HU array in a SimpleITK image with correct spacing and origin.

4. **Builds a StructureSet** — reads 8 structure CSVs (PTV_70/63/56, brainstem, spinal cord, parotids, mandible) and the body mask; creates pyRadPlan VOI objects.

5. **Creates a PhotonPlan** — configures 9 gantry angles (0°–320°, step 40°), 35 fractions, 70 Gy prescription, using the SVDPB pencil-beam dose engine.

6. **Generates beam geometry (STF)** — pyRadPlan computes bixel positions and beam directions for all 9 beams.

7. **Computes the Dose Influence Matrix (DIM)** — runs the SVDPB engine to produce a sparse `(470,000 × 6,292)` matrix mapping every beamlet fluence to dose in every voxel.

8. **Aggregates bixels per beam** — sums all beamlets belonging to the same gantry angle into one column, reducing the matrix from 6,292 beamlets → 9 columns, giving the final `(470,000 × 9)` per-beam DIM.

9. **Sanity check** — applies uniform beam weights (all ones), resamples dose back to the CT grid, and reports mean dose inside PTV_70 (expected ~2 Gy/fraction for 35 fractions × 2 Gy = 70 Gy).

10. **Saves** `pt_1/dim.npy` — float32 array of shape `(470000, 9)`, ~17 MB. Ready to use in an RL environment as `dose = dim @ action_vector`.

|------|-------------|
| `pt_1/dim.npy` | Per-beam Dose Influence Matrix `(470000, 9)` |
